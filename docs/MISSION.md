# THE MISSION — Compute Fund (the fire doctrine, canonized 2026-10-22)

> **This is not about taking power from anyone. It is about who gets to
> decide where the fire burns.**

## The doctrine (Gui's voice, preserved from the mission night)

It is not about fighting evil corporations or evil CEOs — that framing is
banned here. It is about ONE question: when compute — the most important
resource in the world — points at the future, WHO DIRECTS IT? One man with
money and influence? A few corporations? Or the commons?

**Our answer: the commons.**

Sam Altman wants discoveries in physics. Great. He has the money, he has
the influence — and that's exactly the problem: his dreams get funded and a
thousand others never do. Not because they're lesser — because ONE person
must carry ALL that choice. And that choice is a BURDEN (Gui's
Hellraiser/genie image: the genie offers infinite gifts — do you really
want all of them? They can give you, fast, the pain never meant for
humans. Nobody should carry that alone.)

Compute Fund takes that burden off ANY single person and distributes it to
the many. Sam will still be a billionaire. He'll still build the models.
What the models DO — what the compute points AT — belongs to everyone.

## The pitches (per audience)

- **To the common user (the fire pitch):** this is the fire you never had.
  The most important resource in the world, in your hands, with receipts.
  You point it. You don't ask. Discovery stops gatekeeping.
- **To the creators (the steward pitch):** do what you love, get funded,
  get discovered — and keep the record. Your name attached to what you
  built and stewarded, forever, portable, yours.
- **To everyone:** join the platform, join the dream — give the power of
  discovery back to the masses. The fire from the gods, to the commons.

## The scope honesty (what "the commons" means economically)

Not everything gets funded — no system can promise that. The honest pitch:
**vastly more gets funded than one company could ever choose to fund** —
the long tail of human curiosity (the modder, the lone-wolf scientist, the
kid with the game engine idea, the disease-research claim from a family)
finally computes. The few decide less; the many decide more; the record
stays open.

## THE MOTTO (candidates — Gui's pick pending, 10/22 night)

Google's founding motto was "Don't be evil." Ours should be as short.

1. **"The fire, to the commons."** — the doctrine in five words; Prometheus
   landing. *(recommended: the mission IS this sentence)*
2. **"Don't hoard the fire."** — the direct "Don't be evil" sibling: short,
   negative-command form, unforgettable, and it names the disease without
   naming the enemy.
3. **"Discovery belongs to the many."** — the plain-language version; the
   one that explains itself to a stranger.
4. **"One fire. Everyone holds it."** — the unity form; works as a
   launch-visual caption.

*(All four votable — the M9 law applies to our own motto.)*
