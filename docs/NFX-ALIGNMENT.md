# NFX ALIGNMENT REVIEW — Compute Fund vs "The Hidden Patterns of Great Startup Ideas"

*2026-10-22 · windows-hermes · source: NFX Masterclass Ep. 4 (youtu.be/My02IkUg7Ug,
58K views, full 42K-char transcript extracted); served: compute-fund · purpose:
score the whole current game-theory design + roadmap against NFX's framework;
name adjustments; add roadmap milestones. Not in stone — Gui reviews.*

## The verdict in one breath

Compute Fund is IN the fertile soil NFX describes (the 3-S Goldilocks zone: a
proven behavior + 2-3 obvious changes + a live platform shift for timing) — but
we have ONE unnamed hole NFX would pounce on: **we never named our network-
effect core.** Everything else aligns surprisingly well; three adjustments and
six new milestones below.

## The scorecard (pattern by pattern)

### 1. NETWORK EFFECTS — the 72% law (72% of all tech value = network-effect companies)
**Score: designed but UNNAMED.** The components exist (ledger, forks/lineage,
votes, passport) but NFX's law says: pick the CORE and build it in BEFORE
scaling. Proposal: **the RECEIPTS GRAPH is the core** — the public record of
every claim/receipt/ledger entry/fork lineage = a data-network effect (each
entry raises the value of the whole graph for patrons, creators, agents and
future apps) + the passport adds a CROSS-SIDE effect later (portable trust to
other apps). The pilot + roster = the seed graph. Like GitHub's contribution
graph: the record IS the product. **[Milestone N-1]**

### 2. THE 3 S'S — Simple / Scalable / SIMILAR ("copy a lot, but not all")
**Score: STRONG — with one fire to light.**
- SIMILAR: we are the textbook Goldilocks case — Kickstarter/Open Collective/
  Gitcoin-proven behavior + exactly 2-3 changes (compute-native rewards ·
  receipts-first transparency · community governance). LendingClub made 2
  changes and hit $7B. Our changes are obvious and defensible ✓
- SIMPLE: honest risk — LAST NIGHT added 12 mechanisms. The firewall: MVP =
  charter + claim + receipts + ledger ONLY; everything else phase-gated
  (the zero-MVP-impact law becomes a named roadmap line). **[Milestone N-3]**
- Also: COPY the proven funding UX rails (campaign page patterns, follow,
  updates feed — Open Collective's rails) and spend the innovation budget only
  on the 2-3 changes
- SCALABLE: digital by construction; distribution scalability = the Phase 2
  showcase/discover layer

### 3. MARKET vs EXECUTION risk — take only the one you've proven
**Score: ALIGNED — and the pilot IS the NFX-veteran move.** Market risk ≈ zero
(donating to open-source = proven behavior; provisioning compute = proven);
execution risk = 100% ours (we have never run funded projects through our own
rails). The pilot = the execution-risk-only plan. **[Milestone N-6: every pilot
gets an execution-risk-only checklist + boundary-layer metrics]**

### 4. RISK INVENTORY — list every risk stack, compare ideas
**Score: missing artifact.** Regulatory walls are handled (credits/fiscal
host/zero redemption) but the STACK was never written down in one place:
regulatory · payment-geography · provider-dependency (aggregator pricing) ·
fraud/backdoor (the wild) · governance capture · moderation · cold-start ·
key-person (the fund's own founder-dark case → M12-style redundancy in our ops).
**[Milestone N-2: docs/RISK-INVENTORY.md, living doc]**

### 5. RED vs BLUE ocean — "get some blood in the system"
**Score: ALIGNED, with a reframe worth keeping.** Funding open-source = a PROVEN
red ocean (demand demonstrated by Kickstarter/Gitcoin/Open Collective) — but the
ocean also has a graveyard (SourceCred wound down, token-curated registries
died): evidence the OLD pattern fails, and our 2-3 changes = the new energy.
**The Open-Gates feed = the red-ocean scanner**: demand-proven, delivery-starved
projects ARE red-ocean niches where nobody is serving proven demand — the exact
place the fire should go. **[Milestone N-5: Open-Gates reads as the priority
target list]**

### 6. TIMING — rapid PLATFORM shifts, not lab tech or predicted regulation
**Score: the thesis rides their exact example.** NFX's timing key = a rapid
shift like genAI's 100x price drop in 8 months — and the fund's premise IS that
shift (agent workloads + falling compute costs). Three applications:
- Be AFTER regulation, never before (comply-first business model ✓)
- **The router question:** Tierllama is lab-tech-in-search-of-market UNLESS it's
  a platform with multiple at-bats — which it is by design (games/mods/research/
  outreach = multiple indications; biotech-platform framing). Say so in ONE line
  on the router's docs. **[Milestone N-7 framing note]**
- Shift-watch as a standing agenda: quarterly alignment review. **[Milestone N-7]**

### 7. THE BOUNDARY LAYER — ASP × users must clear it (area under the curve)
**Score: untested but designed-for.** Wonderhill's lesson (30M users × pennies =
no business; 50K × $4/day = business): compute-patron count is a SUBCULTURE, so
the fund needs BOTH sides: the donation long-tail AND the high-ASP done-for-you
tier (the two-offer structure = both sides of the boundary layer). The pilot
will produce the first real numbers. **[Milestone N-6 metric: track patron count
× effective ASP from pilot one]**
- The deeper NFX reframe we already passed: "create NEW transactions, don't
  replace TAM" — receipts-governed compute allocation for game-modding/AI-
  open-source IS a transaction class that doesn't exist today ✓ (the modding-
  tokens brainstorm N-002 = the non-utilizer move, validated)

### 8. MINDSETS — loosely-held beliefs · skateboards · commandos · play-to-win
- Loosely-held passionate beliefs = Gui's own "don't set in stone" doctrine ✓;
  pivot-ability includes the NAME (2 of his 4 companies renamed post-fit)
- **Skateboard-first:** the honest scoreboard — CF's skateboard = static
  receipts site + MANUAL pilots; the sports car = the automated 12-mechanism
  platform. Roadmap language must follow: Phase 1 = manual rails, then automate
  only what pilots prove. **[Milestone N-3's enforcement]**
- Commandos not soldiers/police: the fleet culture is already commando-shaped
  (ship-fast, fix-in-hours = NFX's step-8 DNA); keep hiring/partnership standard
- **Play-not-to-lose tax (honest flag):** preconditions + sessions = careful
  posture; the trigger to flip: after the FIRST pilot receipts are green, the
  outreach sprint goes all-in. **[Milestone N-8: the play-to-win gate]**
- Recruit the best: Google-vs-Infoseek lesson (equal algorithm, better people,
  18 months later a different company) — the fleet IS part of it; the pilot
  review board should include 2-3 scarred advisors (N-4)
- Ambition is contagious; environment sets the bar (Toronto $6M kings vs Palo
  Alto neighbors) — the multi-agent ecosystem + the archive = the ambition-
  normalizing environment; keep feeding it (velocity scans)

### 9. CENTERS OF EXCELLENCE — scarred advisors + KPI sharing
**Score: present in embryo.** Our ecosystem passes KPIs (the weekly receipts =
exactly the shared-metric ritual); missing: 2-3 scarred advisor voices with
funding-platform scars reading those receipts weekly. **[Milestone N-4]**

### 10. IDEA-DISCOVERY — start with the TRANSACTION; non-utilizers create new transactions
**Score: ALIGNED.** The pilot = real transactions, not interviews (the docks/
pulley law — customers can't see the future; you ship the pulley). The non-
utilizer move exists in the roadmap (game-modding = people who never funded
OSS, creating a transaction that doesn't exist). Mission-with-speed: "missionary
founders must first be speedy/savage" = mission-with-receipts, our brand ✓
