## THE CORE FINDINGS (what the audit reveals)
1. DEFENSIBLE CORE: "The slow, checkable money." Every adjacent competitor is structurally committed to speed + feeling. Verification takes the time it takes - a funding layer that is deliberately slower is value the fast money cannot copy (their model is incompatible with it). Not just differentiation: an incompatibility moat.
2. Funded security audits as work orders - nobody in open-source funding does "distrust -> pay for an audit -> public receipt." The community heals itself without drama. Product feature + position statement in one.
3. Protect + Belonging combined - funded verification as a community act. Everyone offers one or the other; nobody offers "we all paid for the thing that keeps this safe, and here's the receipt."

## WHERE WE ARE TOO SIMILAR (stop saying these first)
- "We fund open source" = generic; the whole category says it.
- "Deterministic questions" = process talk; people buy the artifact, not the sausage factory.
- The DONATION half of our offer is a commodity (Sponsors does tip jars fine). Donations = the input, not the value.

## THE COMPRESSED PROMISE (landing hero)
"AI made output infinite. What's scarce now is provenance, trust, and care. Compute Fund funds the scarce half - and proves it landed."
(secondary line: "Trust is a product. Bring your receipts." / tertiary: "The slow, checkable money.")
