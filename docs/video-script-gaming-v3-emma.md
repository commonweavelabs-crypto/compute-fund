Hi. I'm Emma. I'm a digital avatar, and I work for Compute Fund. I'm here to tell you
a story — an onboarding story about our platform, and about the gaming side of what
our creators build here.

[01 friendly/steady]
Compute Fund is where creators build in the open, and get funded for it. Every project
carries receipts. What was built, what it cost, what it proved. Patrons can see every
run. That trust is the whole trick. But before I tell you about us, I need to tell you
about something that happened a while back.

[02 contemplative]
In the year 2000, Sony lost a lawsuit. And it mattered, because Sony never loses when
it has the billions, the lawyers, and the most popular console on Earth. The company
that beat them had nine engineers, and one impossible idea. Let Mac owners play
PlayStation games on their computers.

[03 low/controlled]
They took Sony's console software apart to learn how the machine behaved. Then they
wrote their own code from scratch, copying nothing. And the court said, that's how
it's supposed to work. Learning is lawful. Copying is not. Two years earlier, Sega
lost the same fight to Accolade, for the same reason.

[04 resolved]
Here's what that means for our creators. On Compute Fund, rebuilding how a program
works, a decompilation, a recompilation, a port, a compatibility layer, is legitimate
engineering. It has been for over thirty years.

[05 calm/measured]
What's never okay: ripped textures, ripped models, ripped music, someone else's
characters, someone else's trademarks. Make your own. License them. A perfect rebuild
with stolen art is still a violation. And our community is better than that.

[06 warm]
So how does Compute Fund help? Here's the honest answer. We're not a courtroom, and
we don't want to be. Think of our mechanisms as keys. Each one unlocks a piece of the
process, and keeps your work on the safe side of the line the courts already drew.
We guide. We facilitate. And the road gets smoother the clearer you keep it.

[07 calm/confident]
The first key opens every project: the content agreement. One plain page. One
checkbox. I own my rights. The second key is the fork. Our community members never
copy each other. We fork, publicly, with credit, and the original author's line stays
visible in the ledger forever.

[08 steady]
And when you fork, you pay rent. A share of what your fork brings in flows back to
the project you built on. The original creators keep earning. That's the fair share,
written into the machine.

[09 animated]
Here's what we believe. Contribution earns. Do good work and the community rewards
it, and you grow. Everything here is shareable, forkable, and monetizable. Open
source finally has the piece it was missing. Funding.

[10 resolved/warm]
So that's me. Emma, your digital guide to Compute Fund. Open a project. Fork what's
open. Rebuild what you love, clean-room, with your own art. Get funded, on the record.
What a time to be alive.
