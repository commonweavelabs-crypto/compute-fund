# Compute Fund — THE MISSION SCRIPT (YouTube, ~3.5 min voiceover)

*Draft v0 — 2026-10-22, from Gui's mission night. Written to be READ
ALOUD. Emotion first; rules live elsewhere. Gui's phrases kept verbatim
where marked.*

---

**[COLD OPEN — black screen, the sound of fire]**

There's an old story about a theft.

Prometheus stole fire from the gods and gave it to humans. The gods were
furious — because with fire, humans could forge, could heal, could
discover. And could burn.

Fire was never about warmth. Fire was about WHO GETS TO DECIDE WHAT HAPPENS
NEXT.

**[THE TURN]**

We just built the biggest fire in human history.

It's called compute. It's the resource that cures diseases, renders your
dreams into games, proves theorems that stood for a century, and answers —
or will answer — every question we know how to ask.

And right now, almost all of it points where a handful of hands point it.

Not because those hands are evil. Because there are so few of them.

A CEO decides: physics this year. Medicine never. Your idea — the one you'd
burn your life on — doesn't get a hearing, because one person is carrying
every choice at once.

And that choice is a burden. An old myth says genies grant any wish —
infinite gifts. But do you really want ALL of them? They can give you, very
fast, the pain that was never meant for humans. That's what it is to decide
where the world's fire burns, alone.

We don't want to take anything from anyone. **We want to take the burden
OFF everyone.**

**[THE PLATFORM]**

So we built the commons for compute.

It's called Compute Fund. Here's how it works — in one breath:

Anyone — you, me, your cousin, a nurse, a modder, a physicist — can point
compute at what matters to them. Fund a project, fork it, take an idea
somewhere new, buy a single receipt instead of a whole project, or team up
and merge your work with someone you never would have met.

Every action leaves a receipt — timestamped, checksummed, stamped forever.
You can prove what you did. You get credit for what you carried, as a
steward, for as long as you carry it. And when your work is used, the value
routes BACK to you — by receipt, not by favor of a platform.

The discoveries don't pool in one company's vault. They belong to the
people who made them — and to everyone who's next.

**[THE ASK]**

We are not asking you to fight anyone. We're asking you to join the
deciding.

The most important resource in the world — pointed by the many, recorded
by receipts, shared like fire in a village, not hoarded like treasure in a
tower.

Join our platform. Join our dream:

**Give the power of discovery back to the masses.**

*The fire from the gods — to the commons.*

**[END CARD]**

Compute Fund. *The fire, to the commons.*

## Production notes (for the video build)
- The fire motif: match-cuts between Prometheus fire, server racks, GPU
  fans, a child's screen. Gui's Hellraiser-genie beat = a dark beat in the
  middle; keep it short and let the resolution land on the village fire.
- Narration: one voice, low, unhurried. No jargon anywhere (the toggles,
  packs, mechanisms = a different video; THIS one sells emotion only).
- Length discipline: 3–4 min. The rules video ("how it works") ships
  separately.
- His voice line preserved verbatim: "join our platform, join our dream to
  give the power of discovery back to the masses... the fire from the gods
  to the commons — amen."
