## 0. The doctrine (from Gui, 10/22)

- ORGANIC FIRST, paid later: momentum via people, not money; when funds exist,
  hybrid with paid ads (Stage-1's $500 = the first paid test)
- TWO-LANE creators wanted: the fund's favorite partner = someone who BOTH
  builds/host projects on the platform AND makes content to promote it
- SPOTLIGHT SCARCITY: few projects early = "more eyes per project" — the
  first funded creators get the hype; that's the pitch, and it's true
- Claims outreach = EMAIL (we hold creator emails); patron outreach = social
  content (FB/IG/TikTok) via creators
- HONESTY LAWS APPLY EVERYWHERE: the SaaS-review accounts he follows
  (e.g. Starter Story's $X/month formats) = SELF-REPORTED, UNVERIFIED data —
  adopt their FORMATS (story+numbers+funnel), never their looseness. Our
  numbers are receipts; if a partner refuses receipts, that's a no.
