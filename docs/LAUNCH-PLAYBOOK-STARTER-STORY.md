## THE NINE STRATEGIES WORTH IMPLEMENTING (cross-verified, with our mapping)

### S1 · THE CREATOR-FILTERING MACHINE (from #1 — the deepest one)
The playbook: reach out to ~100 UGC creators → *interview* each → ~10% pass the filter (9-10 of 100) → the passing creators make organic videos → any video that crosses ~50K views organically becomes a **paid ad** → check ROAS > 1 → scale, refresh creatives as they fatigue.
**Why it maps to us:** we already hold the creator list (the P2 hunting list). Our Stage-1 ads aren't just "run ads" — the sequence is: *recruit creators → filter by a real conversation (Mom Test rules apply!) → let organic pick the winners → pay only for proven creative.* The X $500 match credit amplifies this at the paid stage. **The receipt-flavored upgrade:** the creators we onboard early become our first receipt-holders — their videos = receiptable artifacts.
→ **Action:** add the creator-filtering funnel to OUTREACH-1's checklist (the interview pass = the pilot interviews already planned — dual-purpose!).

### S2 · THE ONE-CHANNEL CADENCE LAW (from #5 — the most expensive lesson, $0 across 100 apps)
The failure: "I had some followers on X... LinkedIn... Reddit forums... YouTube... I did a lot of things but I didn't really get value from it." Spray-and-pray = zero. The fix: **ONE platform, once a day, documenting the journey** — the cadence IS the strategy. Reddit's honest cost also named: "a lot of work, nurturing, you can't promote."
**Mapping:** our launch presence = ONE channel at launch cadence. Recommendation: **X daily build-in-public** (the receipts screenshots = the native format) OR YouTube weekly — one first, not both. Reddit stays read-only (our existing rule, now cost-confirmed).
→ **Action:** pick the ONE channel before OUTREACH-1 opens; the daily post cadence = a named metric.

### S3 · THE PATTERN-BREAK POSITIONING (from #5)
The contestant cracked LinkedIn by doing **vlog-style content in a place full of launch-videos and expert-interviews** — "a pattern break." Same advice, different format = attention.
**Mapping:** every AI-funding account posts "here's our platform." Our equivalent: **show the receipts on camera** — the metered runs, the checksums, the payouts — a format nobody in the AI-funding space runs. The Investigation Template + the documentary pipeline = our pattern-break content.
→ **Action:** the format rule rides into OUTREACH-1: never explain the mechanism when you can SHOW a receipt.

### S4 · THE CUSTOMER-CALL ENGINE (from #2 — the $69K/2-months secret)
Gaurav: **2,000 customer calls** since start; the first product was "too wide" (an all-in-one marketing tool) → the calls taught the niche → the niche product exploded; a 2,000-person waitlist preceded the launch day hockey-stick.
**Mapping:** confirms the Mom Test milestone gating (watch-list item #2) — the calls ARE the product development. Our pilot interviews = the first ~50 of these. The waitlist-then-launch arc = our launch-day shape (the D3 door is literally this: the waitlist counts who's waiting).
→ **Action:** the interview number gets a target (50 pilot interviews pre-outreach-stage) and the waitlist = the pre-launch metric on the ROADMAP.

### S5 · THE CHURN-AGENT INSIGHT (from #4 — the most important one for OUR product)
Postiz's churn fell 26% → **13.7%** when posting became agentic. Pat's on-air realization: *"churn is typically caused by... some human who decides not to do that human task"* — when the product does the task, the user stops churning.
**Mapping:** our platform's flows (receipts auto-minted, credits auto-routed, discovery auto-run) = the anti-churn design principle. Design every user task to become an agent task ASAP — the user's judgment stays, the paperwork goes.
→ **Action:** product law logged: "every recurring user task we can agent, we agent."

### S6 · THE SHARE-RATE ENGINE (from #6 — the organic monster)
The Geo game: **53% organic share rate**, 98-99% of traffic = word of mouth via group chats. "The product is sharable, that sounds obvious but it's not." Build web-first, zero friction.
**Mapping:** our shareable artifact = **the receipt/result card** — every completed milestone renders a public, linkable, beautiful card (what was claimed, what it cost, what came out). Users share outcomes; outcomes = receipts; receipts = marketing. The "post your score" loop, receipts-style.
→ **Action:** result-cards go on the SPEC-V2 feature list as a launch feature (share = the growth loop).

### S7 · COMPETITOR-KEYWORD ADS (from the reel: the Frederick episode)
Apple Search Ads **bidding on competitor names** → $5K/mo; then "TikTok ads that remade videos already going viral in the niche" → $10K in a week. "The idea barely matters. Distribution does."
**Mapping:** at Stage-1 ads, our bids include the adjacent-category keywords (the AI-subscription plans, GitHub Sponsors, OpenCollective, the cloud GPU rental names). The TikTok remake tactic = the pattern-break content (S3) run as paid.

### S8 · THE SIMPLICITY LAWS (from #3 — the 2-years-stuck survivor)
"I would tell my younger self: skip the complicated system. **Launch the simplest possible version with subscriptions, and listen to users' feedback.**" And the pivot that saved him = the niche (architects, not "everyone").
→ **Action:** already our MVP law — now source-backed. The MVP accepts subscriptions/pre-commitments EARLY (the waitlist → pilot credits → paid lanes).

### S9 · THE DOGFOOD LOOP (from #2 + our own doctrine)
Fastlane's tool makes the viral content that sells Fastlane. Ours: the platform's own artifacts (receipts, result cards, ledger visualizations = Gui's helix video idea!) make the content that sells the platform.
→ **Action:** the launch content = produced BY the platform's machinery, not about it.
